# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publication of others'
private information and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Report concerns
privately through GitHub's repository contact options. All complaints will be
reviewed fairly and confidentiality will be respected.
